this is frist theater

frist campus center   (40.34668, -74.65517)

about this place

small theater with 197 seats on the first floor and 42 in the balcony. most dance groups are given a week to rehearse and perform here, so it's busy for basically the entire semester.

what to do here

spend a week going to rehearsals right after class until late at night and then have fun dancing in the shows. also go to a couple friends' shows and hope your ears don't get blasted off.
