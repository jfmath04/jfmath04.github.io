about this place

cafe known for their japanese kiseki pancakes! they also have chicken, burgers, sandwiches, and other brunch items.

what to do here

go on a date (romantic? platonic?), get one savory item and one kiseki pancake, and split them. then go back again and again to try all the different kiseki pancakes and the seasonal flavors. watch the chefs make the fluffy pancakes while you wait for them to cook.
