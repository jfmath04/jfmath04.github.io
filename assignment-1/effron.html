this is effron

effron music building   (40.34258, -74.65925)

about this place

from the outside this building looks like a bunch of suspended boxes. each box is actually a practice room or an office! every practice room has a nice piano, which sounds kinda expensive. it's also connected by the forum to the rest of the lewis arts complex.

what to do here

go up to the third floor and find a practice room to practice your instrument, or take lessons with a music instructor. or head downstairs to the forum to find lee rehearsal room where the orchestra rehearses every week. perhaps wander around the rest of the lewis arts complex while you're here.
