about this place

colo is a nice eating club with a piano, library, theater, computer room, and game room. there's also a room dedicated to the colonial banking and consulting bros, and a room with framed black and white photos of every class year.

what to do here

claim a table in the dining room for your friend group and do homework on the whiteboard in the computer room. watch a movie in the theater and play minecraft for hours!
